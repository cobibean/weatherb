## ENGAGEMENT TACTICS THAT WORK (Small Accounts)

### Daily Ritual
**Post every day at 8 AM**: "What's your 5-day forecast where you are?"
- Builds habit
- Gathers prediction psychology data
- Creates routine engagement
- Followers expect it

### Reply Loops
When someone replies thoughtfully, continue conversation publicly:
- Makes them feel heard
- Their followers see credibility
- Shows quality of community
- Compounds reach

### Spicy Takes
**1x per week**: A contrarian take on your industry
- "Your weather app is lying to you"
- "Prediction markets will replace weather apps"
- Drives 50-100% more engagement than normal posts

### Quote Tweet Extension
When someone replies with an insight, quote them and ask follow-up:
- Brings their network to see your reply
- Shows you listen
- Drives engagement
- Organic growth

### Week-End Recap
**Every Friday**: Thread summarizing metrics, learnings, what's next
- Tells coherent story for new followers
- Shows momentum
- Invites engagement
- Predictable touchpoint (followers expect it)

## WHAT COULD GO WRONG (Contingencies)

### If Engagement Rate Drops Below 2%
- Content isn't resonating
- Action: Pivot to more educational threads (4-5% engagement baseline)
- Avoid: Metrics-only posts (2% engagement)

### If Follower Growth Stalls
- Network isn't expanding
- Action: Increase daily engagement ritual, tag 3-5 people in replies, post spicy take
- Avoid: Following bots or buying followers (damages credibility)

### If Testnet Signups Plateau
- People interested but uncertain
- Action: Share more testimonials, simplify the offer, add urgency (limited invites)
- Avoid: Pushing too hard (builds skepticism)

### If Settlement Fails
- Data source issue or calculation bug
- Action: Publicly investigate, share fix, build trust through transparency
- Avoid: Hiding the problem (kills credibility forever)

### If Nobody Bets
- Product-market fit issue or user experience problem
- Action: Ask early users directly (DMs), iterate quickly, pivot messaging if needed
- Avoid: Assuming the problem will fix itself
