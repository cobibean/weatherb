# Name Validation & Brainstorming Prompt for WeatherB

## Context: What WeatherB Is (User Perspective)

**WeatherB** is a simple, automated prediction market app where users bet YES/NO on temperature outcomes. Think of it as "the weather app where you can bet on the forecast."

### Core User Experience

**The Product:**
- Users see markets like: "Will it be ≥ 72°F in New York at 2pm?"
- They connect their crypto wallet (WalletConnect/Thirdweb) and place YES/NO bets using FLR tokens
- Markets are automatically created (5 per day) and automatically settled using real weather data
- Users track their positions and claim winnings when they're right
- Community can vote/suggest which cities to add next

**The Value Proposition:**
- **Tagline:** "Call the Temp" — YES/NO bets on weather
- **Hero Message:** "Call the Temp" with subtitle "YES/NO bets on weather"
- **Positioning:** The first accessible weather prediction market. Unlike traditional weather apps (one-way information), WeatherB offers real incentives for accurate predictions, making it "the most honest weather forecast available."

**The Brand:**
- **Design Aesthetic:** "Nostalgic Futurism" — light, airy, minimal UI with sky gradients and cloud textures
- **Tone:** Authentic founder voice, educational, transparent metrics, building in public
- **Target Audience:** Crypto enthusiasts and weather-savvy people who believe prediction markets are more accurate than weather apps
- **Blockchain:** Powered by Flare (FLR tokens)

**Key Differentiators:**
- Simple binary markets (YES/NO only, no complex AMM/orderbooks)
- Fully automated (no manual intervention needed)
- Limited supply (5 markets/day creates scarcity)
- Community-driven expansion (users vote on cities)
- Weather-only focus (not sports, politics, crypto prices)
