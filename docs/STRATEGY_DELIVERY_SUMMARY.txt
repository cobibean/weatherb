================================================================================
  RED FLAG TRIGGERS (When to Pivot)
================================================================================

ENGAGEMENT < 2%
→ Content not resonating
→ FIX: Switch to more educational threads (4% baseline)

FOLLOWER GROWTH STALLS (3+ days)
→ Not enough reach
→ FIX: More engagement loops, spicy take, tag people

TESTNET SIGNUPS < 10/DAY
→ Offer not compelling
→ FIX: Add testimonials, simplify signup, create urgency

0 REPEAT USERS
→ Product unclear or untrustworthy
→ FIX: Improve UX documentation, share settlement proof

SAME 10 PEOPLE ENGAGING
→ Network not expanding
→ FIX: Reply to relevant accounts, quote interesting people

NO UNSOLICITED TESTIMONIALS (by week 3)
→ Product-market fit issue
→ FIX: Investigate why, adjust positioning

If 2+ red flags in same area for 2+ weeks → Call an audible and pivot.
