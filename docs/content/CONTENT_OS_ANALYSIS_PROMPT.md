# Content-OS Analysis Prompt

Use this prompt in your separate content-os repository to analyze the WeatherB strategy against your personal writing style from 169 newsletters:

---

Hi! I need you to analyze a content strategy I just received for a new project (WeatherB) and compare it against my authentic writing voice and style that you've learned from my 169 newsletters.

## Context

I'm launching a new Web3 project called **WeatherB** - a prediction market on Flare blockchain where users bet on temperature outcomes. I hired a content marketing specialist to create a "building in public" Twitter/X strategy to validate the niche over 4-6 weeks leading to a testnet invite code launch.

The strategy is comprehensive and tactical, but I want to make sure it sounds like **me** - not like a generic marketer.

## The Strategy Documents (8 Files)

I'm sharing 8 strategy documents from the WeatherB campaign:

1. **BUILDING_IN_PUBLIC_README.md** - Master navigation and implementation guide
2. **STRATEGY_ONE_PAGER.md** - Week-by-week visual breakdown
3. **STRATEGY_EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md** - Complete strategic overview
4. **CONTENT_STRATEGY.md** - Main playbook with 120+ post examples
5. **TWITTER_LAUNCH_PLAYBOOK.md** - 100+ ready-to-use tweets and threads
6. **READY_TO_PUBLISH_TWEETS.md** - Copy-paste ready content for Week 1-2
7. **METRICS_DASHBOARD.md** - Tracking and validation templates
8. **CLAUDE_PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md** - Instructions for Claude Project

**Strategy Overview:**
- 4-6 week campaign on Twitter/X
- Starting point: 900 followers, zero users, brand new launch
- Goal: Validate weather prediction niche through transparent metrics
- 6 content pillars: Why Weather, Validation Signals, Building in Public, Community Stories, Data-Driven Decisions, Launch & FOMO
- Posting rhythm: 5 posts/week (Monday-Friday)
- Crescendo: Testnet invite code launch in Week 4-5

## What I Need From You

**Analyze these 8 strategy documents through the lens of my authentic voice (learned from 169 newsletters) and tell me:**

### 1. Voice & Tone Alignment
- Does the "founder voice" in the strategy match how I actually write?
- Which parts sound like me? Which parts feel forced or inauthentic?
- Are there phrases, structures, or patterns that don't match my style?
- How would you describe the strategy's voice vs. my newsletter voice?

### 2. Structural & Format Preferences
- Do the content formats (threads, daily rituals, metrics posts) align with how I naturally communicate?
- Would I naturally write in the structures they're suggesting?
- Are there format patterns from my newsletters that should be applied here?

### 3. Topic & Theme Resonance
- Do the 6 content pillars align with topics I naturally gravitate toward?
- Which pillars feel most "me" and which feel like I'm cosplaying as a marketer?
- Are there themes from my newsletters that should be woven into this strategy?

### 4. Authenticity Red Flags
- What specific examples from the strategy feel inauthentic to my voice?
- Are there "marketer-isms" or hype language that I should remove?
- Which ready-to-use tweets sound like they came from a template vs. me?

### 5. Strategic Blind Spots
- Based on how I think and write, what's the strategy missing?
- Are there angles or perspectives I naturally bring that aren't in the strategy?
- What would I talk about that the strategy doesn't address?

### 6. Execution Fit
- Given my writing habits and style, can I realistically execute this 5x/week rhythm?
- Which parts of the strategy will be easy for me vs. forcing myself to write differently?
- How should I adapt the strategy to match my natural writing flow?

### 7. Specific Rewrites
- Pick 5-10 example tweets from `READY_TO_PUBLISH_TWEETS.md` or `TWITTER_LAUNCH_PLAYBOOK.md`
- Rewrite them in MY voice (as learned from 169 newsletters)
- Show me the before/after so I can see the difference

### 8. Integration Recommendations
- How can I blend the strategic framework (good) with my authentic voice (essential)?
- What should I keep from the strategy vs. what should I heavily modify?
- Create a "voice calibration guide" - specific do's and don'ts for making this sound like me

## Key Questions

1. **Does this strategy capture my authentic voice, or will I sound like someone else?**
