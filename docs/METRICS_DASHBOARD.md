## METRICS FRAMEWORK OVERVIEW

The strategy succeeds when you optimize for **three tiers of metrics**:

### Tier 1: Audience Growth (Top Funnel)
What it tells you: Is the concept interesting to new people?
Target: 900 → 5,000 followers in 6 weeks (456% growth)

### Tier 2: Engagement & Community (Middle Funnel)
What it tells you: Are interested people actually engaging?
Target: 3-5% avg engagement rate, 30%+ participation in asks

### Tier 3: Product Validation (Bottom Funnel)
What it tells you: Are people actually using the product?
Target: 50 → 500+ testnet signups, 30%+ retention

## RED FLAG INDICATORS (When to Pivot)

### Growth Red Flags
| Red Flag | Threshold | Action |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| Follower growth < 50/week | If sustained 2+ weeks | Increase engagement tactics, try new content types |
| Engagement rate < 1% | If sustained | Posts are not resonating, audit messaging |
| Follower drop-off (people unfollowing) | If >5%/week | Product is not matching expectation, clarify positioning |
| Negative sentiment in DMs | If >20% of DMs | People think it's a scam or too risky, address fears |

### Product Red Flags
| Red Flag | Threshold | Action |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| Settlement accuracy < 70% | Any occurrence | Fix data source, verify calculation |
| No bets for 2+ days | If sustained | Product visibility issue, increase marketing |
| 0% repeat users | If sustained week | People don't trust product, improve UX/communication |
| Testnet signups < 10/week | If sustained | Offer isn't compelling, adjust incentives |

### Strategy Red Flags
| Red Flag | Threshold | Action |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| Best post engagement drops below 5% | If sustained | Content fatigue, try new formats |
| Same 10 people engaging | If sustained | Network not expanding, increase reach tactics |
| Testnet interest but no bets | If sustained | Product doesn't match expectations, align messaging |
| No unsolicited testimonials by week 3 | Not seeing organic | Product isn't generating word-of-mouth, investigate why |

**When to pivot**: If 2+ red flags triggered in same category for 2+ weeks = call an audible

## STRATEGY ADJUSTMENT FRAMEWORK

### When Engagement Rate Drops Below Target

**Week 1-3 target**: 3-5%
**Week 4-6 target**: 2-4% (larger follower base = harder to maintain high rate)

**If you hit this threshold**:

```
Step 1: Audit content (review last 5 posts)
- Are they about the product? → Likely 2% engagement
- Are they educational? → Likely 4% engagement
- Are they asking for input? → Likely 5%+ engagement

Step 2: Identify what worked (look back 10 posts)
- Which post type got 5%+? [ANSWER]
- Do more of that format

Step 3: Test new format
- If metrics posts underperform, try educational threads
- If threads underperform, try more polls/asks
- If asks underperform, reevaluate audience targeting

Step 4: Adjust daily cadence
- Reduce posting frequency (less = higher engagement)
- Post at different times (test 6 AM, 2 PM, 8 PM UTC)
- Replace low-engagement content types
```

### When Follower Growth Stalls

**If you stop growing followers for 3+ days**:

```
Diagnosis:
[ ] Engagement rate dropped → People aren't engaging, fix content
[ ] Not enough posts → Increase volume to 2-3 per day
[ ] Tweets not reaching new people → Increase reply engagement (grow existing network first)
[ ] Niche isn't as big as expected → Broaden messaging slightly (appeal to finance + weather + crypto)
[ ] No viral/retweet moment → Create 1 spicy take per week

Action:
1. Double down on engagement loops (asks, polls, rituals)
2. Reply to 10+ relevant accounts daily (build relationships)
3. Tag 3-5 people in replies (bring their networks)
4. Post 1 "spicy take" thread (controversial/unique POV)
5. Share 1 quote-worthy insight
```

### When Testnet Signups Slow

**If testnet signups drop below 10/day**:

```
Analysis:
[ ] People don't know what testnet is → Need more explainer content
[ ] People think it's too complex → Simplify pitch
[ ] People want certainty before committing → Share more success stories
[ ] Sign-up process is unclear → Make CTA clearer

Response:
1. Create a 5-tweet explainer thread "What is testnet?"
2. Add 1 user testimonial post per day (if you have them)
3. Simplify signup CTA to single click/link
4. Offer one small incentive (leaderboard spot, early mainnet access)
```

## FINAL NOTE: Metrics Over Vanity

Remember: We're tracking **validation signals**, not vanity metrics.

**Vanity metric**: "I got 10,000 followers!"
**Validation metric**: "34% of people who bet once come back for a 2nd bet."

**Vanity metric**: "My tweet got 200 retweets!"
**Validation metric**: "100 people replied with their weather prediction strategy."

**Vanity metric**: "I have $10K volume!"
**Validation metric**: "9 out of 10 new users placed a 2nd bet within 48 hours."

Focus on the validation signals. They're boring, but they prove something real is happening.
