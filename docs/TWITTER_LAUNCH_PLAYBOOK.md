### Thread: "Prediction Markets 101: The Concept"

```
THREAD: Never heard of a prediction market? Here's how they work. 5 tweets. 🧵

Tweet 1:
"Prediction market: A market where you bet money on the outcome of a future event.

You think X will happen. You buy a share. If you're right, you profit. If you're wrong, you lose.

The market price = the crowd's collective estimate of the probability."

Tweet 2:
"Example:

Event: Will it be ≥72°F in NYC tomorrow at noon?

Market price for YES share: $0.60 (crowd thinks 60% chance)
Market price for NO share: $0.40 (crowd thinks 40% chance)

You buy 10 YES shares at $0.60 = cost you $6.

If YES happens: Your shares worth $10. Profit: $4. ✓
If NO happens: Your shares worth $0. Loss: $6. ✗"

Tweet 3:
"Why do prices move?

As new information arrives, people update their beliefs.

"Breaking news: Warm front moving in" → YES price goes up, NO price drops.

"Forecast just changed to rain" → NO price goes up.

The market price is a real-time reflection of what people believe will happen."

Tweet 4:
"Key insight: Prediction markets are 99% accurate.

Why?

Because if you're wrong, you lose real money. This forces honesty.

The crowd + real incentives = truth-telling mechanism.

This works in politics, finance, sports. Why not weather?"

Tweet 5:
"That's what WeatherB does:
```
